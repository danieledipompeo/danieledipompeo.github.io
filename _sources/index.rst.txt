Daniele Di Pompeo, PhD
====================================

Daniele Di Pompeo is an Assistant Professor (RTD-a) at the University of L'Aquila, Italy, where he earned his Ph.D. in ICT in 2019.
His research spans model-based performance analysis, software refactoring, and search-based software engineering.
He serves as an "Expert of the Subject" in Software Quality Engineering and is an active member of the `SPENCER research group <https://spencerlab-uaq.github.io>`_.
His research has been supported by the European project `SoBigData.it <http://sobigdata.eu/>`_ and is currently supported by the `Vitality <https://fondazionevitality.it/>`_ foundation.
